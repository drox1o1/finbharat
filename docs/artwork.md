# Original artwork

Created with the built-in image generation tool. Final optimised files are stored locally in `public/art/`. Source PNGs remain in the Codex generated images directory.

## Pathways

Landscape editorial risograph: ivory sun, layered teal hills, a winding pale path and a small lavender milestone. Architectural printmaking, fine halftone dithering, dense ink grain and subtle misregistration. Palette: warm ivory #F7F5F0, mint #E6F0EE, teal #114C5A / #102E38, lavender #8B7FB8 and restrained ochre. No text, people, currency or charts.

## Community

Landscape editorial risograph: overlapping rings and circles in an open embrace, small geometric forms and branching connecting paths. Generous negative space, tactile grain, halftone dithering and print misregistration. Same limited brand palette. No text, people, currency or charts.

## Milestones

Landscape editorial risograph: minimalist staircase, arc, rounded hill, lavender sun and ivory arch doorways. Everyday homes and aspirations expressed through architectural geometry. Overprinted shapes, halftone dithering, misregistration and paper grain. Same brand palette. No text, people, currency or charts.
