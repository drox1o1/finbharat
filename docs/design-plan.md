## Multipage revision

The revised design plan was output before writing the new page UI. The deterministic mock remains based on `random.Random(len(user_prompt))`; Figma continues to override any random font or colour selection.

Selected hero architecture: wide editorial split, with the confident three-line homepage headline “Wealth. Simple. / For everyone / in Bharat.” The motto is “Keeping wealth simple. For everyone in Bharat.”

Selected component architectures: paired product gateways; keyboard-accessible learning panels beside abstract visual explanations; editorial cofounder profiles.

Selected motion paradigms: native cross-document page transitions with scoped GSAP entrances and pointer feedback; ScrollTrigger reveals and an expanding explanatory rail. Existing manifesto scrubbing, pinning, image scale/darkening and card stacking remain. Reduced motion removes movement, pinning and scrubbing.

The navigation leads to Mutual Funds, Fixed Deposits, Inclusion and About. Attention comes from the hero and motto; Interest from product explanations; Desire from useful client-side planning and inclusion principles; Action from calculators, learning controls and cofounder links. App availability remains clearly pending.

Homepage and product H1s stay within two or three lines. Bento grids use `grid-flow-dense`. No cheap numbered labels. All action colours retain accessible contrast. Estimates remain visibly illustrative and not financial advice. Founder details use only owner-supplied facts; portraits remain labelled placeholders.

## Cinematic interaction revision

The user requested one version with a full-screen image and centred text. The pre-code mock selection used `seed = len(user_prompt)`: Cinematic Center; inline image typography, horizontal accordion, typographic marquee; pinned split storytelling and overlapping card stacking. The mock font draw (Geist) was overridden by the explicit Figma Noto Sans requirement.

The hero uses `max-w-6xl` (1152px) and exactly two actions. Responsive line breaks produce two desktop lines and three mobile lines. The four intention cards fill a complete 2 × 2 grid, or four mobile rows, with `grid-flow-dense`. The three gallery stories form a sequential stack, not a bento grid. The moving text contains design principles, never invented partner names or testimonials.

The original risograph assets remain the visual source. The hero's minimum 70% deep-teal wash gives ivory text at least 5.42:1 contrast even against a white underlying pixel. Brand-teal action buttons with ivory text measure 9.44:1. Text scrubbing changes between accessible brand colours instead of making words unreadably transparent. Reduced motion removes movement; the principles remain available as static text.
